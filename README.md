## Our Services

### 01 — Web Architecture

> *Fast, scalable websites and web applications built on a solid foundation.*

We design and engineer websites and web applications that load fast, are easy to find and stay easy to grow. Every build starts with architecture (content structure, performance and the integrations your business depends on), so the site keeps working as you scale.

| What we build | |
| --- | --- |
| **Company & marketing websites** | High-performance sites that present your brand clearly and turn visitors into enquiries. |
| **Web applications** | Customer portals, SaaS products and interactive platforms that run in the browser. |
| **Online stores & booking flows** | Product catalogues, checkout and scheduling built around how you sell. |
| **CMS-powered platforms** | Content your team can update themselves, without touching code. |
| **Landing pages & campaigns** | Focused pages for launches, ads and product announcements. |
| **Redesigns & migrations** | Rebuilding slow or outdated sites on a modern, maintainable stack. |

**Included:** responsive design · performance optimisation · SEO-ready structure · accessibility · analytics setup · deployment and hosting

**Ideal for:** businesses launching or relaunching online, teams that need a maintainable web app, companies outgrowing a template or no-code site.

| What we build | |
| --- | --- |

| **Business management systems** | Orders, inventory, approvals and records together in one system. |

### 03 — Agentic Systems

> *AI agents that understand, reason and get real work done.*

We build AI agents and LLM-powered applications that go beyond chat. They read your knowledge, use your tools and complete multi-step tasks, with guardrails, quality checks and a human hand-off wherever it matters.

| What we build | |
| --- | --- |
| **AI assistants on your knowledge** | Answer customer and team questions from your own content, with sources. |
| **Knowledge agents (RAG)** | Search and reason over documents, policies and internal data. |
| **Task-executing agents** | Agents that call your tools and APIs to complete real work end to end. |
| **Multi-agent workflows** | Specialised agents coordinating on complex, multi-step processes. |
| **AI features in your product** | Summaries, data extraction, classification and smart search inside existing software. |
| **Evaluation & guardrails** | Answer-quality testing, safety checks and human review built in. |

**Included:** use-case and feasibility review · data and retrieval pipeline · prompt and agent design · tool and API integration · answer-quality evaluation · monitoring after launch

**Ideal for:** support teams answering the same questions every day, companies with large amounts of internal knowledge, products adding reliable AI features.

### 04 — AI Automation

> *Connected workflows that take repetitive work off your team's plate.*

We map the manual steps in your operations and replace them with reliable automated workflows, connecting your apps, using AI where judgement is needed and keeping a person in the loop for the exceptions.

| What we build | |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead & enquiry handling** | Capture, qualify and route new leads to the right person automatically. |
| **Document processing** | Pull the data you need out of emails, forms, invoices and PDFs. |
| **Reporting automation** | Scheduled reports and alerts assembled from the tools you already use. |
| **App & CRM sync** | Keep your systems in sync without copy-and-paste. |
| **Customer communication flows** | Follow-ups, reminders and notifications sent at the right moment. |
| **Approval & back-office workflows** | Requests, sign-offs and hand-offs that move on their own. |

**Included:** process mapping · workflow design and build · AI classification and extraction · error handling · monitoring and alerts · team training

**Ideal for:** teams losing hours to repetitive admin, businesses juggling disconnected apps, growing companies that want to scale without adding busywork.
